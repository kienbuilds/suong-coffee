**Self-authored under explicit creative delegation.**
User's words, verbatim: "bạn tự quyết hết đi, chủ đề là một website cho một quán
cà phê giá $10000, làm nó thật sống động và chuyên nghiệp".

Evidence vs assumption:
- Evidence (from user): coffee shop; site should feel like a $10,000 build; lively
  (sống động) and professional (chuyên nghiệp); all creative decisions delegated.
- Assumption (authored): the brand. "Sương" is a fictional roaster-café in Hà Nội
  roasting beans from Cầu Đất, Đà Lạt. No real address, no real figures. The page
  says on its face that it is a concept.
- Constraint: no KIE_AI_API_KEY and no user photos, so there is no photography.
  All imagery is drawn in code (SVG, CSS). This pushes the world toward
  technical/illustrated line work, which is the honest choice rather than fake photos.

## The eight topics (authored)

1. **Vibe:** quiet morning, precise craft, warm in the hand.
   References: the opening of *Paterson* (2016) (routine as ritual), a roaster's
   handwritten logbook, a botanical plate from an old field guide.
2. **Journey:** title page with the name and one promise → where the beans come from
   (the hill) → the roast itself, watched in real time → how we brew it → the menu →
   come in, a seat by the window.
3. **Energy:** calm at the open, calmer still just before the roast (an empty
   intertitle), the roast is the one intense stretch, then confident and easy,
   ending quiet.
4. **Feeling and the one moment:** calm → closeness → held breath → delight at the
   crack → trust → appetite → warmth. The moment: the bean cracks.
5. **What no other site does:** you roast a bean with your scroll wheel, and then the
   roast you choose regrades the whole site, including what the menu tells you
   the coffee will taste like.
6. **Range:** editorial, not premium-minimal dark. Paper and ink, folio, captions.
7. **World:** distinct chapters with hard cuts, not one continuous world.
8. **Assets:** round 1 none, everything drawn in code. Round 2: user supplied six photos
   ("hình ảnh không được nhiều và trực quan... tui sẽ cung cấp cho bạn ảnh"). Used four:
   cup + croissant (hero plate, between the type and the front branch), falling beans
   (intertitle, masked into the dark ground), salted-cream cup (menu lead), window seat
   (colophon). Not used: the iced latte (another café's "REFLECT" logo is printed on the cup)
   and the dark-chocolate cup (carries a third-party site watermark). All photos are small
   (365 to 800 px wide), so none is shown full-bleed. Photos join the roast regrade via a filter.
   Grammar note: editorial normally keeps media below the title page; the hero plate
   breaks that at the user's explicit request for more imagery.
   Round 3: user said those photos were found online and the placement looked off; asked me
   to source free photos from Unsplash and redesign. All round-2 photos removed. Eleven
   Unsplash (free licence) photos, listed in CREDITS.md. New photo layout:
   hero = right-half window photo, wordmark crosses the seam (ink on paper, paper on photo);
   ch I = photo pair with a matted inset in front; intertitle = full-bleed roasting photo
   (still the quiet beat: title only, no copy); ch III = tall pour-over photo with wipe +
   inner parallax; menu = one photo per drink, same 4:5 crop and label schema;
   colophon = photo bleeds the left half, reservation plate on the right.
   The roast regrade now also grades every photo.

## Journey (beats)

```
1  Arrival       the name, set like a book's title page, a branch of coffee cherries across it
2  Closeness     the hill in Cầu Đất, drawn as contour lines, small honest prose
3  Silence       a dark intertitle: "II. Rang". Nothing else. (AUTHORED SILENCE)
4  The roast     scroll drives a bean from green to brown; at first crack it splits
5  Trust         how we brew, three recipes, the temperature follows your roast
6  Appetite      the menu as museum labels, travelling sideways
7  Warmth        colophon: hours, a cup filled to your roast, "Giữ chỗ"
```

## Feeling curve

```
1  Calm        type on paper, mist drifting, the branch leaning in over the title
2  Closeness   contour lines of one hill, one paragraph, a caption with a real place name
3  Hush        a full dark screen with a chapter title and nothing else (authored silence)
4  Held breath → delight   the bean darkens under the hand, the curve climbs, then it cracks
5  Trust       plain recipes, numbers you can use at home, the drawing wiped in
6  Appetite    drinks arriving one by one from the right, each labelled with fact
7  Warmth      the smallest type on the site, a full cup in your roast, one line to reserve
```

## Authored silence

Act 3 (`#ch2-intertitle`) is a one-viewport dark intertitle with only the chapter
title. Intentional. Not dead scroll.

## Signature move

**The roast dial.** A three-stop control (Sáng · Vừa · Đậm) in the folio. It regrades
every ground, the bean and cup illustrations, the recommended brew temperature,
and every tasting note on the menu, together. The peak act's curve carries the same
three stops, clickable, so the roast you just watched is where you pick it.
